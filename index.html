About Me

I'm someone who got into AI not because it was trending, but because I genuinely wanted to build things that think. Over the past couple of years I've been doing exactly that — designing agents, wiring up LLM pipelines and making sure they actually work in a real system, not just a notebook.

I built a voice-based agentic system that takes messy spoken input and turns it into clean, structured data — handling hospital appointments end to end without a human in the loop. I also built a multi-agent setup where one agent writes problems, another solves them, and a third checks the math — fully autonomous, self-correcting. These are the kinds of problems I want to keep solving as an AI engineer or agent integration specialist.

On the data side, I'm comfortable taking raw, messy datasets and pulling something meaningful out of them — EDA, feature work, dashboards, the whole pipeline. That's where the data scientist side of me shows up: I care about what the data is actually saying, not just what the model outputs.

I work with Python, LangChain, RAG, FastAPI, prompt engineering, and SQL. I've had internships in backend and data, so I know how AI fits — and sometimes doesn't fit — into production systems. I'm looking for a role where I can grow fast and work on AI that's doing something real.
